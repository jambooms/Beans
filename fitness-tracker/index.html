// Universal Principles — Read First
▼
01When to increase weight
Double progression is the rule. Every lift has a rep range (e.g. 8–10). Start at the bottom. When you hit the top for all prescribed sets with clean form, add the smallest increment (usually 2.5 kg) next session. Reset to the bottom of the range.
02When NOT to increase weight
Do not add weight if form broke down, you needed more than prescribed rest, you didn't hit the top range on all sets, or you're coming off poor sleep. Plateaus are data, not failure. Stall 2 sessions in a row → deload 10% and rebuild.
03RIR (Reps In Reserve)
Every working set should stop with 1–3 reps left in the tank. Heavy compounds: RIR 2–3. Isolations, last set only: RIR 0–1. Training to absolute failure on compounds burns recovery.
04When to take a rest day
Planned: at least 2 full rest days per week. Unplanned — take one if: RHR is 10+ bpm above baseline · slept under 6 hours and feel flat · a joint hurts · you've had 3+ poor workouts in a row. One rest day costs nothing. Injury costs weeks.
05Deload weeks
Every 4–6 weeks: same exercises, same sets, but 60–70% of working weights, stop 4–5 reps short of failure. Fatigue is cumulative — a deload week typically produces a PR the following week.
06Soreness ≠ productive training
Track numbers, not soreness. Progression over weeks is the real signal. Never train a muscle that's still noticeably sore from the previous session.
07Fueling and sleep
Protein: ~1.6–2.0 g/kg bodyweight daily. Pre-workout: carbs + protein 60–90 min before. Sleep: 7+ hours to progress, 8+ ideal.
08Warmup protocol
5 min general → dynamic mobility for target joint → warmup sets: empty bar × 8 → 50% × 5 → 70% × 3 → 85% × 1. Never skip bench warmups.
09Form degradation = stop the set
If the last rep was worse than the previous one — the next rep is a junk rep. End the set. Junk reps build injury risk without meaningful growth stimulus.
10Tracking
Write down weight × reps × sets for every lift, every session. Also note sleep score and energy level (1–10). Progressive overload without tracking is guessing.
